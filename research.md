## Research Summary

My research interests lie at the intersection of natural language processing and machine learning, with a focus on:

1. **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**: Developing methods to improve logical reasoning capabilities in large language models through reinforcement learning techniques.

2. **Vision-Language Model Hallucination**: Investigating and addressing hallucination issues in vision-language models, particularly for chart understanding tasks.

3. **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**: Researching the mechanisms behind LLM truthfulness and developing methods for better interpretability.

My work has been published in top-tier conferences including NeurIPS, ICML, and EMNLP, demonstrating my commitment to advancing the field of natural language processing.
